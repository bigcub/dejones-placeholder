Designing for uncertainty, without making it feel uncertain

Good product work does not remove every unknown. It gives people a calmer way to move through them.

David Jones · Sample note

Layout preview. The copy below is illustrative and has not been published as David's writing.

On this page

  1. Make the uncertainty visible
  2. Choose a useful next step
  3. Leave a record

Make the uncertainty visible

A team can act without knowing everything. It needs to understand which assumptions matter, what evidence is available, and what would change the decision.

Choose a useful next step

A small, reversible step can teach more than a detailed plan built on untested assumptions. Be clear about what you expect to learn before committing more time.

Leave a record

Write down the decision, the evidence behind it, and the questions that remain. That record gives the next conversation somewhere useful to start.
