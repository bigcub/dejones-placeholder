About

The primary aim of this site is to bring clarity to my own thinking after more than fifteen years designing and building software products for the gaming industry. In consolidating my fragmented notes from over the years, and starting with some very raw drafts, I hope to refine them into what will become useful evergreen articles. Should they prove illuminating to anyone who comes across them, then all the better.

The name of the site, Product Thinking, Product Practice, is a nod to one of my old university texts, Peter Checkland’s Systems Thinking, Systems Practice.
